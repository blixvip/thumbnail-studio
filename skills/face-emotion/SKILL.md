---
name: face-emotion
description: Direct a thumbnail portrait with believable expression, useful eyelines, and clear framing.
---

Choose an expression that matches the actual video: curiosity, concentration, delight, concern, or another specific emotion. Do not default to an exaggerated open-mouth reaction. Explain what the person is responding to in the composition.

Use the user's supplied portrait when identity matters. If no portrait is provided, request one or describe a generic model explicitly; never claim an invented face is the creator. Specify the crop, head angle, eyeline, shoulder position, key light, and background separation. Leave room for hands only when they clarify the story and can be rendered naturally.

Preserve recognizable facial features when editing. Check eyes, teeth, ears, fingers, hair edges, and cutout halos. Avoid unnecessary skin smoothing. Deliver a portrait direction, a composition plan relating the face to the supporting object, and a generation or edit prompt. Explain any remaining identity or anatomy issues that need review instead of presenting them as resolved.
