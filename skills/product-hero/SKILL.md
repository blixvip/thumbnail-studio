---
name: product-hero
description: Make a real product, tool, or interface the clear hero of a YouTube thumbnail.
---

Identify the product and the single feature, comparison, or question the video actually covers. Make that detail visually legible instead of packing every feature into the frame.

Use supplied product photos or screenshots for exact shapes, branding, and interface text. If they are absent, explain which assets are needed; distinguish a conceptual illustration from an accurate product depiction. Pick an angle that reveals the relevant feature, give the product enough frame area, and use lighting or a simple backdrop to separate its silhouette.

For software, crop to one useful interface region and remove distracting chrome. Do not invent benchmark results, fake error messages, unreadable dashboards, or endorsements. Provide two layouts, recommend one, and specify object placement, background, optional short text, and any comparison asset needed. Finish with a clear production prompt and checks for product accuracy, edge quality, and small-size readability.
