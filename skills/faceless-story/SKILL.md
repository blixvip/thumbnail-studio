---
name: faceless-story
description: Build a thumbnail narrative through objects, environments, scale, and contrast without a portrait.
---

Translate the video's premise into a concrete visual story that does not require a face. Brainstorm three directions using a revealing object, a meaningful scale contrast, or an environment with one unusual but relevant detail.

Choose one focal object and at most two supporting cues. State what the viewer should notice first and which question follows. Keep the imagery tied to the video's real subject; do not substitute a generic robot, glowing brain, money pile, or explosion simply because it looks dramatic.

Define camera position, object scale, depth, lighting, negative space, and optional exact text. Use metaphor only if the relationship to the topic is immediately understandable. Distinguish a conceptual scene from documentary imagery. Recommend the strongest concept and deliver a detailed generation prompt. Check object plausibility, confusing symbolism, texture clutter, and whether the idea survives without its title at small size.
