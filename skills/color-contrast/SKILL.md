---
name: color-contrast
description: Improve thumbnail subject separation, tonal hierarchy, and restrained color relationships.
---

Start by identifying the subject and the existing palette, using attached images if provided. Diagnose brightness contrast separately from hue contrast. Do not assume saturated color automatically makes a thumbnail clearer.

Choose a restrained palette: one dominant color, one supporting neutral, and at most one accent tied to the story. Provide hex values for graphic areas, but describe natural skin tones and lighting in photographic terms. Keep the brightest or highest-contrast region on the intended focal point. Reduce distracting background detail and highlights before adding effects.

Specify changes to subject exposure, background luminance, edge separation, text contrast, and accent placement. Describe a grayscale check and a small-size check. Avoid relying on red-versus-green distinctions alone. Deliver prioritized editing instructions and a focused image-edit prompt. Preserve recognizable product colors and realistic skin unless the brief explicitly calls for a stylized treatment.
