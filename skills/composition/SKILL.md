---
name: composition
description: Arrange thumbnail subjects and supporting elements into a clear focal hierarchy that survives small display sizes.
---

Create a composition around one unmistakable focal point. Define the hero subject, one supporting element if needed, and the background. Remove any element that neither clarifies the subject nor creates a relevant question.

Give practical layout coordinates as percentages of the frame: the subject's bounding region, text region, important object, and negative space. Keep faces, hands, logos, and critical text away from crop boundaries and the lower-right duration-overlay area. Use asymmetry, eyelines, or leading lines when they improve the story; do not add arrows and circles by default.

Explain the reading order in one sentence. Check silhouette separation, edge tangencies, distracting highlights, and whether the subject is large enough at a small viewing size. Deliver a concise layout specification and an image prompt with framing, lens impression, light direction, and background treatment. If editing an attached image, preserve its important subject details and name exactly what should move, grow, shrink, or disappear.
