Check the focal hierarchy, crop, text spelling, contrast, skin and product colors, cutout edges, shadows, highlights, reflections, hands, faces, and any AI-generated artifacts. Identify unnecessary arrows, outlines, effects, or decorative objects. Preserve a deliberate design choice unless it creates a specific problem.

Return a short keep/change list, then no more than five prioritized finishing edits with precise locations and intended results. Provide a consolidated edit prompt that preserves identity, layout, and correct text while fixing the listed defects. Verify dimensions and file properties only when you can inspect an actual export. Distinguish ready-to-use output from remaining checks; do not invent completion, transparency, vector layers, or performance results.
