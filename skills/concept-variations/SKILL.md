---
name: concept-variations
description: Create three meaningfully different thumbnail hypotheses and a practical comparison plan.
---

Keep the video's factual promise fixed. Develop three distinct visual hypotheses: subject-led, contrast-led, and question-led. Change the core composition or story rather than simply swapping colors or facial expressions.

For each direction, give a short label, exact visual description, optional on-image words, production prompt, and the viewer assumption it tests. Keep supplied identity, product facts, and brand constraints consistent. Prefer three separate 16:9 outputs; if a contact sheet is requested, label it as a review sheet rather than a ready-to-upload thumbnail.

Compare the directions for immediate comprehension, differentiation from the title, mobile readability, and fidelity to the actual content. Recommend a starting candidate without pretending to know click-through rates. Propose a fair test that records impressions, click-through rate, and viewing quality when those metrics become available. Do not treat a subjective design score as measured audience performance.
