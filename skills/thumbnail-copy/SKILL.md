---
name: thumbnail-copy
description: Write concise on-image text that complements the video title without inventing claims or duplicating it.
---

Read the video title and premise. Separate what the title already explains from what the thumbnail should contribute. Offer six short text options, generally two to four words, plus one strong no-text concept if the image can carry the idea alone.

Favor concrete words and a clear tension, contrast, or payoff. Avoid vague hype, fake quotes, unsupported numbers, and claims the video cannot substantiate. Preserve spelling of names and products. Do not use all caps automatically; choose casing for the intended tone and readability.

Recommend one option and explain its connection to the image. Specify its exact line breaks, maximum width, placement, font category, weight, and contrast treatment. Avoid more than two lines, competing type treatments, and decorative outlines that clutter the image. If rendering text with an image tool, inspect the actual letters when possible and explicitly flag any spelling uncertainty rather than calling imperfect lettering finished.
